**Previewing your new App Store and Google Play version** before upload catches
the mistakes that are expensive to fix once a build is in review: the wrong
screenshot order, a missing localized subtitle, a description that overflows.

## New vs Current, side by side

The tool keeps two variants:

- **New** — the version you're about to upload. Fill it with **Try template**,
  *Load images*, or drag & drop, then edit the text on the page.
- **Current** — what's live now, pulled from the stores via Sync.

Switch between them to see your changes in a realistic **App Store** and **Google
Play** layout — including the iPhone, iPad, and Android phone galleries, and every
localized field.

## Review the diff before you ship

**Review Diff** compares Current → New in one dialog:

- **Text & metadata** — per-locale field changes (name, subtitle, description…).
- **Screenshots** — slot-by-slot visual comparison, with a fullscreen lightbox.

Anything added, removed, or changed is flagged, so nothing slips through.

## Try it

Open the [Playground](/playground/), build your New version, and hit **Review
Diff**. For the full walkthrough see [Usage](/guides/usage/) and
[The integrated site](/guides/the-site/).
